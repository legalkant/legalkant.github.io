Study Resources

Structured legal-learning resources for LLB students, revision and academic preparation.

Constitutional Law

Preamble, Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles, Fundamental Duties, constitutional institutions, judicial review, amendments and basic structure.

Contract Law

Proposal, acceptance, consideration, capacity, free consent, legality, void agreements, performance, breach and remedies.

Law of Torts

Negligence, nuisance, trespass, strict and absolute liability, defamation and vicarious liability.

BNS — Criminal Law

Study offences, general exceptions, abetment, attempt and offence-specific statutory ingredients.

Open Bare Acts

BNSS — Criminal Procedure

Arrest, investigation, complaints, bail, inquiry, trial, appeals and procedural safeguards.

BSA — Evidence

Relevancy, admissions, confessions, documentary and electronic evidence, burden of proof and witnesses.

Property Law

Transfer of property, sale, mortgage, lease, exchange, gift and related property-law doctrines.

Company Law

Incorporation, share capital, directors, meetings, management, accounts, audit and corporate compliance.

Consumer Law

Consumer rights, product liability, unfair trade practices, consumer commissions and dispute redressal.

Jurisprudence

Nature of law, rights, duties, possession, ownership, liability, legal personality and jurisprudential schools.

CPC & Civil Procedure

Jurisdiction, pleadings, summons, issues, judgments, decrees, execution, appeals, review and revision.

Administrative Law

Delegated legislation, natural justice, judicial review, administrative discretion and legal remedies.

Study Method

Read the Bare Act first. Mark definitions, exceptions and important provisions. Prepare section-wise notes, then study leading judgments and solve practical legal problems.
