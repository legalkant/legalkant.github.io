Legal Drafting

Practical drafting resources and document structures for law students and legal learners.

Legal Notice

Pre-litigation communication

Learn the structure of a legal notice, including facts, legal basis, demand, response period and consequences of non-compliance.

Reply to Legal Notice

Response drafting

Understand paragraph-wise responses, admissions, denials, factual corrections and appropriate legal positions.

Affidavit

Sworn statement

Study the basic structure of an affidavit, verification and attestation requirements applicable to the relevant proceeding.

Plaint

Civil litigation

Study parties, jurisdiction, cause of action, limitation, valuation, reliefs and verification under applicable procedural law.

Written Statement

Civil defence

Learn preliminary objections, paragraph-wise responses, admissions, denials and additional pleadings.

Consumer Complaint

Consumer Law

Learn how to organise consumer status, transaction documents, allegations, jurisdiction and reliefs.

Legal Agreements

Contract Drafting

Study parties, definitions, scope, consideration, obligations, confidentiality, indemnity, termination and dispute resolution.

Legal Opinion

Legal Advisory Writing

Use the structure: Instructions → Facts → Questions → Applicable Law → Analysis → Conclusion.

Drafting Principle

Templates are starting points only. Every legal document must be adapted to the actual facts, applicable law, forum requirements and current amendments before use.
