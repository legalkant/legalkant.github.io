Legal Research

A practical research framework for law students and legal learners conducting Indian legal research.

01

Define the Legal Question

Convert the problem into a precise legal question. Identify the jurisdiction, relevant facts, date and area of law.

02

Find the Governing Law

Start with the Constitution, applicable Acts, Rules, Regulations, notifications and amendments.

03

Read the Relevant Sections

Read definitions, provisos, explanations, exceptions and schedules along with the main provision.

04

Research Case Law

Search judgments using the statutory provision, legal issue, party names and citations. Always verify the actual judgment.

Supreme Court Search
05

Check Amendments

Confirm that the statutory text and relevant case law are current and check amendments, commencement notifications and related rules.

06

Analyse the Authorities

Identify the ratio decidendi, relevant observations, factual distinctions and subsequent treatment of the judgment.

07

Prepare Research Note

Use the structure: Question → Short Answer → Law → Authorities → Analysis → Counter-arguments → Conclusion.

08

Verify Citations

Verify case name, citation, court, date, statutory provision and paragraph references before using an authority.
