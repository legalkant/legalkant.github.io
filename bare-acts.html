A curated educational collection of important Indian legislation for law students and legal researchers, with links to official government sources.

Important: LegalKant provides these resources for educational and research purposes. Always check the current official text, amendments, notifications and commencement provisions before relying on any legislation for a legal matter.

🇮🇳 Constitution

Constitution of India

Enacted: 26 November 1949
Commenced: 26 January 1950

The supreme constitutional framework of India, containing provisions relating to fundamental rights, directive principles, the Union and States, judiciary, elections, emergency provisions and constitutional amendment.

Enforced: 1 September 1872

Defines and amends important parts of the law relating to contracts, including proposals, acceptance, consent, competency and contractual obligations.
